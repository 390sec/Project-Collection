# **Project: Cinema for Truth & Understanding**

## **Purpose**

To build a collection of films and documentaries that help in **understanding reality through clear evidence and structured observation**.

This is not for entertainment.
This is for **truth, clarity, and real-world understanding**.

---

## **Principle**

Only include content that:

* Shows **real systems, facts, or evidence**
* Helps **understand how the world actually works**
* Can be **analyzed and verified**

---

## **Exclude**

Avoid content that is mainly:

* Emotional stimulation (love, desire, fear without insight)
* Escapism or fantasy without real-world relevance
* Addictive or attention-driven media

---

## **Method**

For every film/documentary:

1. Identify the **core truth or system shown**
2. Separate **reality vs dramatization**
3. Extract **clear, usable insight**
4. Keep notes short and evidence-based

---

## **Outcome**

* Clear thinking
* Reduced distraction
* Better understanding of reality

---

## **Final**

Cinema is not truth.
But it can be used to **observe, question, and approach truth**.
