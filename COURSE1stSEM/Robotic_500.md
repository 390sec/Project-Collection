### Real recommendation

From 500 available:

* shortlist **50–100 potentially useful**
* keep **20–30 per archive**
* only retain what matches your learning roadmap

Because robotics spans:

* mechanics
* electronics
* control theory
* AI
* computer vision
* optimization
* embedded systems
* simulation

So “500 robotics books” is a category dump, not a learning plan.
