I'm teaching from Noah Stephens-Davidowitz's lecture notes. I recommend reading these notes.

Other references:

Rafael Pass, Abhi Shelat, A course in Cryptography (These notes are also very good).

MIT course website
Video lectures of the MIT course: 2018, 2021, 2025 (I have watched the 2018 lectures, they are good. Not sure about other years).

Mike Rosulek, The Joy of Cryptography

Jonathan Katz and Yehuda Lindell, Introduction to Modern Cryptography

Oded Goldreich, Foundations of cryptography, Volume 1 (This book is difficult to read. I won't recommend reading it in the beginning. I will mention in the class which topics to read from this book).
